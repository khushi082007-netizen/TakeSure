TakeSure — Demo Video Script & Production Guide

CodeSlayer 2.0 2K26 · Round 1 (Online Ideation) · Team Bug Hunters · Target length 2:45–3:05
The one rule that decides everything. The organisers wrote: "this round is for idea presentation only. If we find that a team has already built a prototype or working solution, that team will be disqualified." So this video explains the idea, the design and the plan. It never shows a running website, an app screen, a screen recording, code, a URL or a QR code. Everything is said in the future or conditional tense: "the user will photograph…", "the system will check…", "in the 36 hours we will build…". Never "we built", "we tested", "our prototype shows", "it already works".

"Step two, Match: Indian brand names become generics — Thyronorm becomes levothyroxine — and products become their herbs, so Chyawanprash maps to amla, ashwagandha and the rest."

"Step four, Explain: only now does AI come in. It rewrites the matched evidence into simple Hindi or English and drafts three questions to ask the doctor. Step five, Report: a one-page PDF for the doctor, and a suspected-reaction report pre-filled in the Ministry of Ayush format."

4. How to shoot and edit it (free tools, one evening)

Recording

  • Phone camera, landscape, 1080p, 30 fps. Clean the lens.
  • Face the window or a lamp; never have the light behind you. A plain wall behind you is best.
  • Audio matters more than video: use wired earphones with a mic, or record in a quiet room 30–50 cm from the phone. Switch off fans and phones.
  • Record each segment separately, 2–3 takes each. Read from a teleprompter app (e.g. "Teleprompter" on Android/iOS, or BIGVU free) with the script above pasted in.
  • Speak a little slower than normal and smile at the camera at the start and end of each take.
  • For the hook, hold the strip and the bottle at chest height so both are in frame.

Editing

  • Clipchamp (already on Windows 11) or CapCut (free): one track for the slide images, one for the camera clips.
  • Export each PPT slide as a PNG (PowerPoint → File → Save As → PNG → "All Slides"). Use them full-screen; place the speaker in a circle or rectangle in the bottom-right corner (picture-in-picture).
  • Add captions / subtitles — Clipchamp does this automatically. Judges often watch on mute.
  • Soft background music at 10–15% volume only; none under the hook.
  • Export MP4, 1080p. Name it BugHunters_TakeSure_CodeSlayer2K26.mp4.
  • Upload to Google Drive → Share → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer. Open the link from a friend's phone to confirm it plays.

5. Compliance checklist — tick every box before uploading

  • Length between 2:30 and 3:30.
  • At least one face clearly visible (yours: all three appear).
  • No app screen, website, screen recording, code editor, terminal, URL or QR code anywhere in the video.
  • No sentence that says or implies the product already exists ("we built", "it works", "our prototype", "live", "deployed", "users tested"). Replace with "will", "would", "we plan to", "in the hackathon we will build".
  • Numbers match the PPT exactly (37%, 65%, 19%, 1,473).
  • Team name spelled the same way everywhere: Bug Hunters.
  • Drive link pasted on slide 1, sharing set to Anyone with the link, tested from another phone.
  • PPT re-exported to PDF after pasting the link; PDF is what you upload on Unstop.

6. Judge questions you may get later — one-line answers

QuestionAnswer
Isn't this just ChatGPT for herbs?No. The verdict comes from a versioned table with sources; the model only rewrites the matched row and is schema-locked. Switch it off and the verdict is identical.
What if the herb or medicine isn't in your database?We say "Not in database" and never guess. Every miss is logged as a curation task.
Who is liable if the advice is wrong?It is decision support: we never say stop or start; we show evidence and questions for the doctor — the same posture as Medscape or Drugs.com.
OCR on Indian handwriting?The user confirms every name before any lookup, and manual entry is always available.
How does the Ministry benefit?Structured ICSRs in its own format, co-use analytics by state, and signal detection — feeding the existing NPvCC and Ayush Suraksha system, not duplicating it.
Evidence is weak for many herbs.Exactly why every result shows an A/B/C grade. Weak evidence shown as weak is itself information a patient never had.
Business model?Free for citizens; API for pharmacies, hospital systems and telemedicine; Ministry programmes and CSR from Ayurvedic brands; ABDM integration later.
